# 📊 India Unemployment Rate Analysis (COVID-19 Impact Study)

## 📌 Project Overview
This data science project investigates the macroeconomic impact of the COVID-19 pandemic and subsequent nationwide lockdowns on India's labor market using monthly unemployment records from **May 2019 to June 2020**. 

The dataset was cleaned, transformed, and segmented into three distinct time horizons (**Pre-COVID**, **COVID-19 Lockdown Peak**, and **Post-Lockdown**) to evaluate regional vulnerabilities, urban vs. rural disparities, and temporal spike clustering.

---

## 🔍 Key Findings & Analytical Insights
* **Macro Lockdown Shock:** National average unemployment experienced an abrupt, near-vertical surge past **25%** during the designated April–May 2020 lockdown peak[cite: 4].
* **Urban vs. Rural Disparity:** Urban labor markets recorded a higher overall average unemployment rate (~13%) compared to rural areas (~10.3%), peaking near **28%** due to service sector closures and informal daily-wage disruptions[cite: 1, 2].
* **100% Peak Clustering:** 100% of the top 30 highest unemployment spikes in the dataset occurred exclusively within the March–May 2020 lockdown timeline[cite: 7].
* **Regional Outlier Hotspots:** Extreme unemployment rates (ranging from 40% to 70%) were heavily concentrated in specific states, most notably **Haryana, Jharkhand, Bihar, Tripura, and Puducherry**[cite: 5, 7].

## 💡 Policy & Strategic Recommendations
1. **Targeted Regional Relief:** Focus fiscal stimulus and job-creation packages specifically on vulnerable outlier states rather than relying solely on uniform national policies[cite: 3, 5].
2. **Urban Safety Nets:** Establish portable social security schemes and direct benefit transfers for urban informal and daily-wage laborers[cite: 2].
3. **Phased Containment:** Implement localized, micro-containment protocols during health crises to prevent catastrophic nationwide labor collapses[cite: 4, 7].
